Status: manual structure ready. Automatic money note creation and retrieval are planned work in [[context-memory]].

Money notes track obligations Mark needs Marcus to remember: invoices, collections, recurring costs, payment promises, and financial risk.

## Capture Standard

Each money note should include:

- who owes or is owed
- amount, due date, status, and source record when available
- related project, client, or person
- latest collection or payment update
- risk level and next action

Never store card numbers, bank details, API keys, account passwords, or private payment credentials.
